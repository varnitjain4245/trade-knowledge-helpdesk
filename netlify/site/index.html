Ministry of Commerce & Industry · prototype

Trade Knowledge Helpdesk

Ask about export licensing, customs, GST, MSME schemes or e-commerce rules. Every answer quotes the record it came from. When nothing covers your question, the desk says so rather than guessing.

Speak your question and hear the answer. Say “stop” to end.

How this works. Answers are drawn only from an indexed set of records, and each is quoted with its issuing authority and date. Where a model drafts the wording, the draft is checked against those records first and discarded if it says anything they do not support.

Prototype for the Smart India Hackathon. The records, rates and scheme figures are illustrative — check the published notification before acting on a figure.
